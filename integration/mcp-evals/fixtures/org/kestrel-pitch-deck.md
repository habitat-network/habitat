# Kestrel — Pre-seed Pitch Deck (October 2026)

## 1. The one-liner
Kestrel is the shared memory for warehouse floors: every shift, supervisor and
agent works from the same record of what happened, what's broken and what was
promised.

## 2. Thesis
The warehouse floor's real operating knowledge lives in people's heads and in
group chats. Warehouse management systems (WMS) record inventory; nobody records
the exceptions, workarounds and promises that decide whether orders ship on
time. As AI agents start doing operational work, whoever owns that context
owns the floor.

## 3. Problem
- Shift handoffs happen over WhatsApp and whiteboards. Night shift doesn't know
  what day shift promised a customer.
- Exceptions (damaged pallets, short picks, mis-slotted SKUs) are handled
  one-off and never learned from.
- Supervisors spend 6–9 hours a week reconstructing "what happened" for
  customers and managers.

## 4. Product
- **Exception Inbox:** every floor exception captured once, routed, resolved and
  searchable.
- **Shift Handoff Notes:** structured handoffs generated from the day's
  exceptions, read and acknowledged by the next shift.
- **Floor Agent:** answers "what happened with order 4471?" from the shared
  record, and drafts the customer update.

## 5. Ideal customer profile
Third-party logistics providers (3PLs) running **2 to 10 warehouses**, 50–400
floor staff, on a legacy WMS they won't replace. Buyer: VP of Operations.

## 6. Traction
- **6 paying pilots, $41k ARR**, all converted from free trials in under 30 days.
- Northgate Logistics expanded from 1 to 3 sites in September.
- Median supervisor saves 5.5 hours/week (pilot survey, n=23).

## 7. Why now
LLMs can finally turn messy floor chatter into structured records, and 3PL
margins are under pressure from rising labor costs.

## 8. Competition
Legacy WMS add-ons are inventory-first. DockPilot is the closest point
solution but is single-tenant and expensive. Kestrel is the only product built
around exceptions and handoffs rather than inventory.

## 9. Business model
$450 per warehouse per month, annual contracts.

## 10. The ask
Raising a **$2.4M pre-seed** to reach 25 paying 3PLs and $350k ARR in 18 months.
Use of funds: 60% engineering, 25% go-to-market, 15% operations.
