# Decision: Pricing for general availability

Date: 2026-09-18. Decided by: Dana Whitfield, Sam Okafor, Leah Brandt.
Status: Decided.

## Decision
- **$450 per warehouse per month**, billed annually.
- No monthly plans. Annual contracts only, with a 60-day pilot-to-paid window.
- **The free tier is dropped** for new customers. Existing free sites keep
  access until 2027-01-31, then convert or lose access.
- Volume discount: 10% off at 5+ warehouses, 15% at 10+.

## Options considered
1. Per-seat ($25/user/month). Rejected: floor staff turn over too fast, and
   seat counts punish the customers who roll out widest.
2. Per-warehouse, monthly. Rejected: churn risk in pilots, and cash flow.
3. Per-warehouse, annual (chosen).

## Rationale
- Per-warehouse matches how 3PLs budget (by site).
- DockPilot's price increase leaves room: at $450 we are roughly 35% cheaper per
  site for a typical customer.
- The free tier brought in single-site teams that never converted.
