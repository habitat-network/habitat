# Kestrel Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Ops. Effective 2026-03-01.

## Remote-first
Kestrel is remote-first. You can work from anywhere in the US or Canada. Tell
People Ops before working from another country for more than two weeks.

## Core hours
Be reachable **10am to 2pm Mountain Time**, Monday to Thursday. Fridays have no
meetings.

## In-person weeks
The whole company meets in person **one week each quarter in Denver**. Travel
and lodging are covered. Customer site visits are scheduled around these weeks
when possible.

## Equipment
- New hires get a laptop and a **$1,200 home office stipend**.
- Coworking memberships are reimbursed up to $250/month.

## Customer sites
Customer success and solutions staff visit warehouses regularly. Site visits
follow the customer's safety rules: steel-toe boots and hi-vis vests are
provided.
